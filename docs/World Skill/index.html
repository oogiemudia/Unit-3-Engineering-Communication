Unit 3 CAD hub

World Skill

The WorldSkills UK Mechanical Engineering: CAD competition. Its published practice paper, the 2022 part modelling speed test, gives four parts to model in one hour. All four are here, modelled to the sizes on the competition sheets.

The competition papers, 2020 to 2026

WorldSkills UK has published one practice paper for this competition in those years: the 2022 Pre-Passive Activity: Part Modelling Speed Test (1 hour, "create 3D models for all of the parts shown on the part drawings"). The 2025 and 2026 technical handbooks still point to it. The National Qualifier and Final papers for each year are not published.

The handbooks also describe two older sample projects (a 2019 National Qualifier engine and a 2018 National Final packaging machine), but they give only a picture and the task list, with no part drawings, so they are not modelled here.

What each part has

Each part is built feature by feature as in Autodesk Inventor (the Build steps tab), with third angle, first angle and section drawings, a STEP file and turntable, views and section videos. Section A-A on each hub drawing is the section on the competition sheet (A-A, C-C, D-D or E-E). Where a sheet leaves a size out, the size was scaled from the sheet and the build step says so.

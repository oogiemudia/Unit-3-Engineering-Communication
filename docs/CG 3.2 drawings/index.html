Unit 3 CAD hub

CG 3.2 drawings

Every part drawn in the City and Guilds Unit 3 resources for criterion 3.2 (PowerPoints 7, 8 and 9 and Worksheets 13, 14 and 15), built as a real CAD model. Each one is built step by step as in Autodesk Inventor, so beginners can see the drawing, then the part, then how it is made.

How to use these

Show the City and Guilds drawing first, then open the model: spin it, then switch to Front view, Plan and End view to see where each view of the drawing comes from.

Play the build video (or open Build steps and press Next step) to show how it is made, one Inventor command at a time. Each step names the command, where it is on the ribbon, and the sizes typed in.

The three fit models are made at the nominal size, as in Inventor: the fit comes from the tolerance classes on the drawing, not from modelling the hole and shaft different sizes.

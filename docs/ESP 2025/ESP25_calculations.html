Calculations for the preferred design (Design A): carousel pill dispenser

1. Assumptions

  • Sizes and masses come from the CAD model (models/esp2025.py): all 37 parts are modelled with their own material density; the dispenser has 54 parts in all.
  • The largest pill in the brief: a cylinder 5 mm across and 20 mm long, 1.3 g/cm³ (0.51 g).
  • Twice a day dosing is the worst case for the battery. The patient presses the button within 3 minutes of the alarm on average.
  • Labour £17 an hour (brief). Material and bought in prices are supplier estimates for batches of 1500 to 3000; moulding at £28 an hour of machine time with its operator share.
  • Friction: pills and PP carousel on the PP deck μ = 0.3; acetal worm on acetal gear μ = 0.25.
  • Prying force on the lid 200 N: a strong two handed pull at the front edge by someone trying to get at the pills.

Target IP67 (30 minutes under 1 m of water); each unit gets an air leak test at the end of the line. Only the compartment over the drop hole is ever open to the drawer, the worm cannot be back driven, and the lid needs the pharmacy key, so the patient can only ever take the dose that is due.

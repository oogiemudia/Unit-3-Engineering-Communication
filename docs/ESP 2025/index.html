ESP 2025: automated pill dispenser

T Level Design and Development Employer-Set Project, 2025, Task 2. WBEP Ltd is designing an automated pill dispenser for a health trust: no bigger than 190 x 190 x 60 mm, no heavier than 0.5 kg with its batteries, at least 3 pills in each compartment, refilled every 28 days (once a day) or 14 days (twice a day), an alarm that sounds until the pills are dispensed, a PRESS HERE button beside a flashing light, tamper proof with a lock only the pharmacy can open, waterproof, a recyclable casing, and £65 a unit for 1500 (or £40 for 3000). Everything for the project is in this one folder.

Task 2 submission set

Part A (two annotated 3D design ideas): sheet ESP25-GA-04, with the calculations (size and mass, capacity, battery life, the drive, the lid catch, cost and the most units each budget buys, programme, every assumption) in their own document. Part B (the preferred design, dimensioned, with a title block): sheets ESP25-GA-01 to 03, the parts list ESP25-GA-05 and a drawing for every one of the 37 parts (ESP25-P01 to P37), with the reflective notes (about 500 words) in their own document.

How it goes together: 6 sub-assemblies

As in Inventor, the parts are first put together into sub-assemblies (each its own .iam), and the six are then placed into the dispenser with the four cover screws. Each one below has its own 3D model with an exploded view, a parts list, and Inventor build steps that add the parts in order. The dispenser's own Build steps then place the sub-assemblies in order; the drawings ESP25-GA-06 (exploded) and GA-07 (structure and order) show the same on paper.

Every part (37)

Each component is its own part, as it would be in Inventor (a .ipt file): its own 3D model, Inventor build steps, drawing with a key sizes table, and STEP file. Numbers are the item numbers on the parts list, ESP25-GA-05.

How it was made

Every model is parametric CadQuery Python in models/esp2025.py in the repository: change a number (the case size, the number of compartments, the gear) and rebuild; the mass, capacity and costs are worked out again from the parts. The Inventor build steps are in models/esp2025_steps.py, the dimensioned sheets in tools/esp2025_drawings.py, the calculations and notes in tools/esp2025_calcs.py, and the clash and floating checks in tools/esp2025_checks.py. Build steps and the simulation are written as the Autodesk Inventor commands a learner would use; the stage models are CadQuery, not Inventor files.

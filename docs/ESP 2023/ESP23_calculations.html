Hydrostatic pressure the lock gates must resist

1. Data and assumptions

  • Opening 4.2 m wide, gate height 6.0 m, head of water 2.0 m to 5.5 m (brief). Design for the worst case: 5.5 m on the chamber side and the downstream side empty.
  • Fresh canal water: density ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s², so ρg = 9.81 kN/m³. The water is still (hydrostatic): no waves or surge.
  • Mitre gates: two leaves 2460 mm long (pivot to mitre) meeting at 1 in 3 (θ = 18.4°). Each leaf takes the water on its own length.
  • Steel S355 (yield 355 MPa, E = 210 GPa). Pintle 316 stainless (yield 250 MPa used). Self weight from the CAD model.
  • Gates only move when the levels are within 50 mm of each other (control interlock).

2. Pressure at depth

p = ρgh. At the cill with 5.5 m of water: p = 1000 × 9.81 × 5.5 = 53.95 kPa. With the minimum 2.0 m head: p = 19.62 kPa. The pressure is zero at the surface and grows in a straight line to the cill, so the pressure diagram is a triangle.

The two leaves act as a three pin arch: the water pushes each leaf against the other at the mitre and back into the quoin. That is why a mitre gate points upstream: the higher the water, the tighter the mitre and quoin seals.

5. Bottom girder (worst girder)

Carries the water on the strip from the cill to half way to the next girder (0 to 0.475 m): w = ρg (h z − z²/2) = 24.5 kN/m. Span between the heel and mitre posts L = 1.76 m. Treated as simply supported, which ignores the arch action and so is on the safe side: M = wL²/8 = 9.49 kNm.

Section: 400 mm of skin plate acting with the girder, web 20 mm, flange 200 × 20 mm. A = 17160 mm², neutral axis 13 mm from the leaf centre, I = 534 × 10⁶ mm⁴. σ = M y / I = 9.49 × 10⁶ × 238 / 534 × 10⁶ = 4.2 MPa, safety factor 84. OK. The girders are governed by stiffness, the axial thrust from the mitre (577 kN shared by eight girders) and boat impact, not by bending stress.

7. Drive (electric actuator)

With the levels equal to within 50 mm, the most water push on a moving leaf is 9.81 × 0.05 × 5.5 × 2.46 = 6.64 kN at mid length, a torque of 8.2 kNm. Pintle friction (μ = 0.1 on a 75 mm radius) adds 0.65 kNm, and water drag at 0.05 m/s is negligible. Design torque 10 kNm. The actuator lever arm about the pivot is 0.85 m shut and 0.90 m open, so the actuator needs 12 kN. Stroke 1256 mm (pin centres 3301 to 2045 mm): a 20 kN, 1300 mm stroke electric actuator is chosen.

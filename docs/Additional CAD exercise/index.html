Unit 3 CAD hub

Additional CAD exercise

Fifteen exercise drawings, modelled in 3D: textbook sheets, tutorial drawings in millimetres and inches, two piping isometrics, an engine oil pan, an engine block deck and a helicopter concept sketch. The exercise sheet "Uygulama 8, 9, 10" holds six parts, so there are 20 models: 15 single parts and 5 assemblies.

What each model has

Each model is built feature by feature as in Autodesk Inventor (the Build steps tab), and each assembly keeps every component as its own part, with a parts list. Every model has third angle and first angle drawings (and a section where it helps), a STEP file, turntable and views videos, and an Inventor Stress Analysis of one part checked by hand. The five assemblies that move (roller, machinist's jack, planetary gears, pipe run with valves and helicopter) also have a Dynamic Simulation.

Inch drawings are converted to millimetres (1 in = 25.4 mm). Where a drawing leaves a size out, or two of its sizes disagree, the size used is given below and in the build step that uses it.

Crankshaft, four cylinderCigüeñal (crankshaft), sheet 4 of 7The 72 and 78 diameters on the sections are taken as the round ends of the webs; the webs are 15.5 thick ((76 - 45) / 2).
Crankshaft, three throwThree throw crankshaft (inches)The R169 crown on the web faces and the R0.64 edge rounds are left off; the webs are flat 19 mm plates with R2.54 edges.
V-belt pulley (inch drawing)V-belt pulley (inches)The groove root diameter (4.75 in) is read as 5.25 in less two lots of 0.25 in, as the section shows.
Pipe run with three gate valvesPipe run with valvesThe drawing is an AI sketch and its sizes do not agree (for example two 750 runs, a 250 flange on a 70 pipe). Kept: pipe 70 diameter, riser 850, runs 650 and 750, three valves. The middle valve is drawn with two handwheels; the model gives it one. Flanges are DN65 sized (165 diameter, 4 x M16).
Steam line loop (piping isometric)Sample piping isometric with a bill of materialsThe isometric is a teaching poster and some of its callouts and BOM lines do not agree. The model keeps the BOM sizes and counts; it adds two 8 inch elbows, two 8 x 6 reducers and the flange bolting, which the loop needs but the BOM leaves out. The gate and needle valves are butt welded, so the only flanges are the two on the ball valve.
Engine oil panEngine oil pan (overall inch sizes)The drawing gives only overall sizes (21.13, 9.29, 8.62, 10.55, 1.93, 5.85, 6.60, 7.57, 7.67 in). The wall, bosses, filter boss and drain sizes are typical values, not from the sheet. The cross ribs on the side are left off.
Engine block deckEngine block deck plan (inches)Only the deck plan is given, so the depth (180), the water jacket and the coolant holes are typical, and the cast water passages shown as irregular shapes on the deck are simplified to round holes. The head bolt holes fall inside the water jacket, so solid bosses carry them through the coolant.
Light helicopter (concept)Helicopter concept sketchThe sketch has no usable sizes (its numbers do not match each other), so the model is to proportion only, at a light helicopter scale: cabin 1.6 m, overall 8.8 m, rotor 10.4 m. The cabin is a revolved pod; doors, windows and details are left off.

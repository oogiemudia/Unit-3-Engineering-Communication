Unit 3 CAD hub

GrabCAD models

Complete assemblies of the kind people share in online CAD libraries such as GrabCAD: vices, bearings, gearboxes, joints and mechanisms. Every one is our own design, modelled from scratch in CadQuery, so it can be changed and rebuilt and none of it belongs to anyone else.

What each model has

Every component is its own part, with a parts list. Each model has third angle, first angle and section drawings, and Autodesk Inventor build steps: every part feature by feature, then the assembly a few parts at a time. It also has a turntable and an exploded video, an Inventor Stress Analysis of one part checked by hand and, where something moves, a Dynamic Simulation.
